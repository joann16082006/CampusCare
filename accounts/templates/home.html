About

Why CampusCare?

In many colleges, complaints about classrooms, hostels, transport and academics are made on paper or by word of mouth. They get lost, students never know if anyone is working on them, and there is no record of who handled what. CampusCare puts the whole process online, so every grievance is tracked from submission to resolution.

Features

What you can do

Submit online

Report a problem in under a minute, by category.

Track status

See when your grievance is Pending, In Progress or Resolved.

Staff dashboard

Staff see every complaint and update it in one place.

Secure and private

Students see only their own grievances. Every change is recorded.

How it works

Three simple steps

1

Student submits

Choose a category and describe the problem.

2

Staff take action

Staff move it to In Progress and work on it.

3

Issue resolved

The student is notified when the status changes.
